THE WORLD IN ONE DAY
WEDNESDAY, 18 NOVEMBER 2026
Middle East

Iran Strikes Back in the Gulf

US jets struck an Iranian Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island on Tuesday. Early Wednesday, Iran fired drones and missiles at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain; both countries say their air defences intercepted them.

Ukraine

Kerch Bridge Hit Again

Ukraine struck the Crimea (Kerch) Bridge for a second day; part of its road span collapsed into the strait. Ukraine also hit two Russian oil refineries overnight.

Diplomacy

Xi and Putin Meet Before Trump Summit

Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wednesday ahead of Thursday’s first three-way summit with Donald Trump at APEC. Xi pledged to stand against trade protectionism; Trump arrived in Shenzhen and was greeted by Xi.

United States

Democrats Take the Senate

Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican Senator Dan Sullivan in Alaska, the last undecided Senate race; Democrats take a 56–44 majority, their largest since 2009.

Health

Ebola Passes 11,000 Cases

The Ebola outbreak in DR Congo has more than doubled in three months to over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths, the UN said; nearly three in four infected children under 5 do not survive.

Science

Voyager 1, One Light-Day Away

Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth on Wednesday at 5:16 a.m. ET — about 16 billion miles — a distance no human-made object has crossed before, NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory said.
